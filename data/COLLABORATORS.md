# International Collaborators — sources and maintenance

Verified on 2026-10-09. The Members section includes 81 currently verified faculty collaborators at institutions outside mainland China, including Hong Kong and professors emeriti. Each has a genuine coauthored catalog entry with Hai-Ning Liang. This is a verified roster, not a claim that every coauthor is a faculty member.

## Display order

Frequent collaborators (8 or more joint catalog entries) and a small editorial selection of established HCI/XR field leaders appear first; each group sorts by current joint-publication count, then name. The `featured` flag records this selection and `priority_source` links the profile used for review. It is not an h-index or numerical influence ranking. Every build recalculates counts and order.

## Content and images

- Edit `data/international-collaborators.json`; do not edit the generated cards.
- Current appointments are verified from the linked official or self-maintained academic profiles. School names retain their official spelling in all languages; roles, countries and actions are translated.
- Portraits come from the source pages recorded per person. The checked files are stored locally; large originals are exported as small WebP assets without changing their content.
- 69 portraits are available. The 12 unavailable portraits use initials; no stock images, generated faces or unrelated profile photos are used.
- Search links retain the existing `q` parameter. Exact collaborator names and their aliases match verified author membership, so short names do not match unrelated titles/authors. Original bibliographic authors are unchanged.
- Counts represent catalog entries, including coauthored journal articles, conference/workshop papers, preprints, chapters and editorials. They do not count a book’s `List of Contributors` as joint research. A preprint and its final version can be separate catalog entries.
- External collaborators do not become lab members and do not change the member/alumni author-highlighting rules.
