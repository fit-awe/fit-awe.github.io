Research at HKUST(GZ)

HKUST(GZ) is a research university in Guangzhou, China. The Computational Media and Arts (CMA) Thrust brings together computing, media, and the arts.

Research Assistant Positions

We welcome applicants familiar with VR/AR/MR or game development in Unity or Unreal, or hardware design. A commitment of at least four months is expected.

Apply by email with your CV and transcripts.

Postdoc Positions

Email Prof. Liang with your research background, interests and CV. Use “Application Postdoc” as the email subject.

Application materials

Send your CV, research interests and relevant work; include transcripts for student and research assistant applications.

hainingliang@hkust-gz.edu.cn

If you do not receive a reply, your research background or interests may not align with the lab’s current research focus; as our time is limited, we may be unable to respond to every inquiry, and we appreciate your understanding.
