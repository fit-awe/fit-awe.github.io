Hai-Ning Liang 梁海宁

Associate Professor · Associate Head of Research & Outreach

Computational Media and Arts Thrust
The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

Dr. Hai-Ning Liang is an Associate Professor and Associate Head of Research & Outreach in the Computational Media and Arts Thrust at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). He serves as the Vice-Chair of the University Human Subjects Panel and is the current President of the Greater Bay ACM SIGCHI Chapter.

Previously, he was a Full Professor and the Founding Head of the Department of Computing at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, where he also established and served as Deputy Director of multiple key laboratories, including the Suzhou Municipal Key Lab for Intelligent Virtual Engineering and the Suzhou Key Lab for Virtual Reality Technologies.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science from Western University, Canada, where his dissertation was nominated for the Governor General’s Gold Medal. His research pioneers novel interaction paradigms, especially in immersive technologies (VR/AR/MR), data visualization, and gaming technologies.

He has authored over 350 peer-reviewed publications, accumulating more than 9,000 citations and an h-index of 51. Consistently recognized on the Stanford-Elsevier World’s Top 2% Scientists List in both the Career-long and Single-year categories, his work has earned numerous accolades, including the IEEE ICDM 10-Year Highest-Impact Paper Award and Best Paper Honorable Mentions at premier venues such as ACM CHI, IEEE VR, and IEEE ISMAR.

A dedicated leader in the global HCI/XR community, Dr. Liang serves as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, Behaviour & Information Technology Journal, and Frontiers in Virtual Reality, and holds prominent organizing roles for top-tier conferences, including Program Co-Chair for VINCI’25/27, Associate Chair for ACM CHI’27 and Program/Poster Co-Chair for IEEE VR and IEEE ISMAR.
