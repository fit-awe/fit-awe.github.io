Computational Media and Arts Thrust
The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am an Associate Professor and Deputy Thrust Head of Computational Media and Arts at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), where I lead the FIT-AWE Lab.

World’s Top 2% Scientists ↗
Stanford–Elsevier · 2026 edition, single-year impact

I received my PhD in Computer Science from Western University, Canada. Before joining HKUST(GZ) in 2024, I was a Professor of Computing at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, where I served as Head of the Department of Computing (2019–2023) and Deputy Director of the Suzhou Municipal Key Lab for Intelligent Virtual Engineering and the XJTLU Virtual Engineering Centre.

I serve as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics and Behaviour & Information Technology Journal, and as an Editorial Board Member for ACM Interactive Surfaces Spaces – PACM HCI. I also contribute to the organization and program committees of ACM CHI, IEEE VR, IEEE ISMAR, VINCI, ICXR and IEEE AIxVR.
